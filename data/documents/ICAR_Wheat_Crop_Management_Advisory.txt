ICAR WHEAT CROP MANAGEMENT & DISEASE ADVISORY MANUAL
Document Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal
Official Portal: https://icar.org.in

1. YELLOW RUST (PILA RATUWA) DIAGNOSIS & REMEDIATION
Yellow rust (Puccinia striiformis) is a serious fungal disease affecting wheat in Northern Plains.
Symptoms: Bright yellow pustules appear in linear rows along the veins on leaf blades. Upon touching, yellow powder sticks to fingers.
Chemical Remedy: Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml per litre of water (200 ml per acre in 200 litres of water) or Tebuconazole 250 EC immediately upon symptom appearance. Repeat spray after 15 days if rust spreads.
Resistant Varieties: HD 2967, DBW 187 (Karan Vandana), PBW 550, HD 3086, DBW 222.

2. ZINC & NITROGEN DEFICIENCY (YELLOWING OF LEAVES)
Symptoms: Pale yellowing of upper or younger leaves without pustules or powder.
Remedy: Foliar spray of 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (21% Zn) + 1.0% Urea (dissolve 1 kg Zinc Sulphate + 2 kg Urea in 200 litres of water per acre). Apply basal dose of 25 kg/hectare Zinc Sulphate during field preparation.

3. FERTILIZER SCHEDULE FOR WHEAT
Recommended NPK Ratio: 120 kg Nitrogen (N), 60 kg Phosphorus (P2O5), and 40 kg Potash (K2O) per hectare.
Basal Application (At Sowing): Apply 50 kg DAP + 30 kg Urea + 20 kg MOP per acre at the time of sowing.
Top Dressing: Apply remaining Urea in two splits: 30 kg/acre at 1st irrigation (Crown Root Initiation - CRI stage, 21 days after sowing) and 30 kg/acre at 2nd irrigation (Jointing stage, 45 days after sowing).

4. IRRIGATION SCHEDULE
Wheat requires 5-6 irrigations depending on soil texture. Critical stages for irrigation:
1. Crown Root Initiation (CRI) - 20-25 days after sowing (Most Critical)
2. Tillering stage - 40-45 days after sowing
3. Jointing stage - 60-65 days after sowing
4. Flowering stage - 80-85 days after sowing
5. Milk/Grain filling stage - 100-105 days after sowing
