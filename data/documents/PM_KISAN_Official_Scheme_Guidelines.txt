PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN) - OFFICIAL SCHEME GUIDELINES
Document Source: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
Official Portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW & BENEFITS
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme providing income support to all landholding farmer families across the country to enable them to take care of expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs. Under the scheme, financial benefit of Rs. 6,000/- per per annum is transferred directly into the bank accounts of farmer families in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000/- every four months.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
All landholding farmers' families, which have cultivable landholding in their names are eligible to get benefit under the scheme.
Exclusions: High net-worth individuals, institutional landholders, former and present holders of constitutional posts, former and present Ministers/ State Ministers, Mayor of Municipal Corporations, Chairperson of District Panchayats, serving or retired officers/employees of State/Central Government, all superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000/- or more, and income tax payers in the last assessment year.

3. HOW TO REGISTER (NEW FARMER REGISTRATION)
Farmers can apply online by visiting the official PM-KISAN portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) and selecting 'New Farmer Registration'. Alternatively, registration can be done through the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or State Nodal Officer (Agriculture).

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR REGISTRATION
- Aadhaar Card (Mandatory for identity verification)
- Bank Account Passbook (Bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar and NPCI mapping enabled for Direct Benefit Transfer)
- Landholding ownership document (Khasra/Khatauni copy showing land title)
- Active Mobile Number for OTP authentication and status updates

5. MANDATORY e-KYC & AADHAAR SEEDING
To receive future installments without interruption, all registered farmers must complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC. e-KYC can be done via OTP authentication on the PM-KISAN Portal/App or using biometric authentication at any CSC center. Bank accounts must be linked with Aadhaar to avoid payment failures.
