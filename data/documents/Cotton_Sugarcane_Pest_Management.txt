1. COTTON PINK BOLLWORM (GULABI SUNDI) MANAGEMENT
Pink bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) damages developing bolls, leading to poor lint quality and premature boll dropping.
Integrated Pest Management (IPM):
- Install Pheromone Traps @ 5 to 8 traps per acre for pest monitoring.
- Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 4 grams per 10 litres of water (80g/acre) or Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 ml per litre of water.
- Avoid late spraying of synthetic pyrethroids which induce secondary pest outbreaks.

2. SUGARCANE RED ROT (LAAL SADAN) DISEASE
Colletotrichum falcatum causes Red Rot in sugarcane. Internal tissues of stalks turn red with white cross-bands and alcohol-like fermented odor.
Remedial Protocol:
- Uproot and burn infected clumps immediately.
- Treat seed setts before planting in Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2 grams per litre of water for 30 minutes.
- Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 150 ml per hectare for Top Borer control.
