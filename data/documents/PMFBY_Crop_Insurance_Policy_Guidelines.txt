PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY) - CROP INSURANCE POLICY GUIDELINES
Document Source: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt of India
Official Portal: https://pmfby.gov.in
Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-1551

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE & COVERAGE
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest stage (drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, pests & diseases, landslide, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, and cyclone).

2. PREMIUM RATES FOR FARMERS
Farmers pay a nominal uniform premium rate for all crops insured:
- Kharif Crops (Food grains & Oilseeds): Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured
- Rabi Crops (Food grains & Oilseeds): Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured
- Commercial & Annual Horticultural Crops: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured
The balance premium is subsidized equally (50:50) by the Central Government and State Government.

3. MANDATORY INTIMATION TIME PERIOD FOR CROP LOSS CLAIM
In case of localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, natural fire) or post-harvest losses, intimation of loss must be reported within 72 HOURS of the event.
Intimation Channels:
- PMFBY Crop Insurance App (Download from Google Play Store)
- Toll-free Helpline Number: 1800-180-1551
- Nearest Bank Branch / Insurance Company Office / Agriculture Officer
- CSC (Common Service Center)
Documents required for claim settlement: Land title passbook, Bank passbook copy, Aadhaar card, and photos of damaged crop field.
