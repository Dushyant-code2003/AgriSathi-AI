PRADHAN MANTRI KRISHI SINCHAYEE YOJANA (PMKSY) & MICRO-IRRIGATION GUIDELINES
Document Source: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt of India
Official Portal: https://pmksy.gov.in

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE: 'PER DROP MORE CROP'
PMKSY focuses on enhancing water use efficiency at farm level through Micro-Irrigation systems like Drip Irrigation (Tapkan Sinchai) and Sprinkler Irrigation (Fawwara Sinchai).

2. ADVANTAGES OF DRIP & SPRINKLER IRRIGATION
- Drip Irrigation saves 40% to 50% water compared to conventional flood irrigation.
- Enables Fertigation: Water-soluble fertilizers can be injected directly to plant root zones, saving 25-30% fertilizer cost.
- Higher yield (20-30% increase) in horticultural crops, vegetables, banana, sugarcane, citrus, and cotton.

3. SUBSIDY PATTERN
- Small & Marginal Farmers (Up to 2 hectares land): Up to 80% to 90% subsidy on micro-irrigation equipment installation cost.
- Other Farmers: Up to 45% to 55% subsidy.
Application Procedure: Farmers can apply online on their respective State Horticulture Department / Agriculture Department portals or visit the District Agriculture Officer.
