KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) SCHEME - RULES & APPLICATION GUIDELINES
Document Source: National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) & RBI
Official Portal: https://nabard.org

1. PURPOSE & SALIENT FEATURES
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme aims at providing adequate and timely credit support from the banking system to farmers for their short-term crop production needs, post-harvest expenses, produce marketing loan, consumption requirements, and maintenance of farm assets & activities allied to agriculture (dairy, fishery, poultry).

2. CREDIT LIMIT & LOW INTEREST BENEFIT
- Maximum collateral-free loan limit up to Rs. 1.60 Lakh (extendable up to Rs. 3 Lakh with flexible security).
- Benchmark Interest Rate: 7% per annum.
- Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI): Interest subvention of 3% per annum is granted to farmers who repay their loan on or before the due date, reducing the effective interest rate to ONLY 4% per annum.

3. ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENTATION
Eligible Beneficiaries: Individual farmers, joint borrowers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and Self Help Groups (SHGs) / Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).
Documents Required:
- Duly filled KCC Application Form
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / PAN)
- Address Proof (Aadhaar / Ration Card)
- Landholding Proof (Khasra / Khatauni document signed by Revenue Authority)
- Passport-size photographs
Application Locations: Any Commercial Bank, Regional Rural Bank (RRB), State Cooperative Bank, or Common Service Centre (CSC).
