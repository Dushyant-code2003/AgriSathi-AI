1. SOYBEAN CROP PROTECTION & INSECTICIDE PRICE INDEX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Major Pests: Tobacco Caterpillar (Spodoptera litura), Semilooper (Illi), Stem Fly, Girdle Beetle, Green Semilooper.

Recommended Treatments & Price Comparison:
1. Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG (Proclaim / Benzo)
   - Category: Chemical / Bio-rational
   - Target: Tobacco Caterpillar, Semilooper (Illi)
   - Dosage: 80 gram / acre (in 150-200 litres water) or 0.5 g/L water
   - Market Price: ₹350 - ₹450 / acre (₹400 for 100g pack)
   - Category Pick: 🌟 BEST VALUE & BUDGET PICK (High knockdown for young caterpillars)

2. Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% w/w SC (Coragen / Cover)
   - Category: Chemical (Systemic Anthranilic Diamide)
   - Target: Heavy Semilooper & Spodoptera caterpillar outbreak
   - Dosage: 60 ml / acre (in 150-200 litres water) or 0.3 ml/L water
   - Market Price: ₹850 - ₹1,050 / acre (₹1,500 per 150ml bottle)
   - Category Pick: 🛡️ PREMIUM PICK (Long residual protection: 21 days)

3. Flubendiamide 39.35% M/M SC (Fame / Takumi)
   - Category: Chemical Diamide
   - Target: Leaf folder, Green semilooper, Spodoptera illi
   - Dosage: 50 ml / acre or 0.25 ml/L water
   - Market Price: ₹650 - ₹800 / acre
   - Category Pick: ⚡ HIGH SPEED KILL PICK

4. Bacillus thuringiensis var. kurstaki (Bt Bio-Insecticide)
   - Category: Bio-Insecticide / Organic
   - Target: Early instar caterpillars (Illi)
   - Dosage: 400 g / acre or 2 g/L water
   - Market Price: ₹250 - ₹350 / acre
   - Category Pick: 🌱 100% ORGANIC & SAFE FOR BEES PICK

5. Neem Oil 1500 PPM (Azadirachtin)
   - Category: Organic / Preventive
   - Target: Egg masses, early nymphs, caterpillars
   - Dosage: 5 ml / L water (1 litre / acre)
   - Market Price: ₹200 - ₹300 / acre
   - Category Pick: 🌿 PREVENTIVE ORGANIC PICK
