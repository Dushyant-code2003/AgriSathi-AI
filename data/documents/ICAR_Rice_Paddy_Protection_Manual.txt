ICAR RICE & PADDY CROP PROTECTION MANUAL
Document Source: ICAR-National Rice Research Institute (NRRI), Cuttack
Official Portal: https://icar-crri.res.in

1. RICE BLAST DISEASE (MAGNAPORTHE ORYZAE)
Rice Blast is one of the most destructive diseases in paddy cultivation affecting leaves, nodes, and neck of panicles.
Symptoms: Spindle-shaped/eye-shaped brown lesions with grey/white centres on leaves. Neck blast turns panicles dark and causes grain filling failure.
Chemical Treatment: Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 grams per litre of water (120g/acre) or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/litre of water.
Cultural Management: Avoid excessive Nitrogen application. Keep water level at 2-3 cm during peak tillering stage. Use resistant varieties like Pusa Basmati 1121, PR 126, or Swarna Sub-1.

2. SHEATH BLIGHT (RHIZOCTONIA SOLANI)
Symptoms: Oval or irregular greenish-grey water-soaked spots on leaf sheaths near water line.
Remedy: Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 2 ml/litre or Validamycin 3% L @ 2 ml/litre.

3. BROWN PLANT HOPPER (BPH) PEST CONTROL
Symptoms: Circular patches of dried/browned plants in the field known as 'hopper burn'.
Remedy: Spray Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 120g/acre or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 80g/acre. Alternate irrigation to dry field periodically.
